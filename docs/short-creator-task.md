VISUAL RULES, how to enforce them in the build. The rules themselves are section 6 of the Rules page; this block is the build-side procedure for them. These win over the shot list, the guardrails and the README. Applying them is never a block; mention what you changed in the review message.
1. No slowed clips. The renderer slows a clip that is shorter than its line. After make_props, check every line: (clip seconds after its start) must cover the line's seconds in timeline.json (+0.6 s tail on the last line). If not: start the clip earlier, or split the line over two clips ("clips"/"starts"), first from the same day, then from past days in the catalog (_library). Recheck until every line plays at 1.0x.
2. No on-screen text that repeats what the voice says (the captions already show every word). Drop CLIP tags and card items that restate the spoken line. On-screen text only for info the voice does not give (kcal, protein, deficit, kg), keeping estimate marks (≈, EST).
3. Nothing on line 1 except footage, the DAY badge and captions. Never DAY N or the day type in a tag or label.
4. No black screen, ever. No full dark cards: every line has footage. A row with no clip gets a same-day clip first, else a past-day clip from the catalog that fits without misleading (Kent's face or reaction, gym, kitchen). Never show another day's food as this day's meal. Proxy it from _library like any other clip.
5. Number panels stay on screen 4 s or more: merge the panel's line with the line before or after it in script.json (one entry, joined text), then re-run align. Related numbers in one panel; deficit always with totals. The totals card cannot show estimate marks, so totals go in a text panel labeled "TODAY (EST)" with the ≈ numbers.
6. Every move and meal named is shown when footage exists. A MONTAGE row (several clip ids) becomes {"clips": [...], "starts": [...]} in script.json, in spoken order. Split the line's time so each clip appears while its move or meal is spoken (use the word timings from align when available, else equal shares, each clip at least 0.6 s and at 1.0x). If the voice names a move or meal that has a same-day clip the shot list did not use, add it to that line. A clip that shows a move or meal is never used as background for a line that does not name it; swap it for a neutral clip of the same moment.
